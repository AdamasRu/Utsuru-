Bitmap images were extracted directly from referenced pages of X_all.pdf. Original embedded image data is retained in bitmap_assets_native. For images with a PDF soft mask, bitmap_assets_display includes a PNG composite that restores the source transparency without retouching or cropping.
The index maps each image to material ID, PDF physical page, PDF xref, and pixel dimensions.
The bundle includes bitmap objects at least 100x100 pixels, plus eight full-page PNG reproductions for vector-only kana/kanji references.
PDF physical page numbers are 1-based.
